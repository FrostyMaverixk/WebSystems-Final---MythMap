A FEW NOTES FOR THE JOURNEY

Explore the atlas

  • Find a tale. Search the collection or filter by land, sea, and community stories. Select a creature to read its story. Zoom in to see the island terrain. Choose Enter 3D view to explore the creature in its habitat.
  • Explore the map. Drag to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom. With the map focused, use arrow keys, + / −, and Home to reset.
  • Share a story. Sign in as an explorer or admin, select “Share a myth,” then choose a location on the map. Press Escape to cancel.
  • Keep your favorites. Sign in and save a story from its reading panel. Use the Saved filter to find it later.
  • Guest access. Browse every published tale without credentials. Sign in whenever you want to contribute or save stories.

Map locations are illustrative. Folklore varies across communities; credit your sources when sharing.
